HOKIPO · Home & Living DTC Category: Home Transformation

Scaling Past Catalog Fatigue: Building a 14-Ad BAU Creative Matrix Across 5 Product Lines

The Account Bottleneck

HOKIPO was experiencing severe ad fatigue on standard white-background product catalog ads. Ad spend was stalling, and blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) was creeping up. They needed evergreen (BAU) creative angles that converted cold traffic without discounting.

The Strategic Intervention

I analyzed customer feedback and forum chatter to uncover the real pain point: people had rock-solid wooden sofa frames, but the fabric was stained and tired. A carpenter quoted ₹18,000 to reupholster. We built the "Reupholstery Cost Arbitrage" and "Anti-Retuck Stability" angles across 5 product lines (Sofa Covers, Cushions, Mattress Protectors, Dining Chairs, Recliners).

BERKOWITS CLINIC / 360 · Personal Care & Clinical DTC Category: Skeptic Conversion

Overcoming High-Skepticism Markets Using AI-Generated B-Roll & Hinglish Hooks

The Category Challenge

Hair thinning and visible grey roots are high-friction, sensitive buying decisions. Traditional polished clinic ads looked like pharmaceutical infomercials—viewers scrolled past immediately, producing poor 3-second hook rates (<18%) and high CPCs.

The Strategic Intervention

We identified the "Salon Gap" anxiety: grey roots showing 2 hours before a wedding or meeting when there's no time for a salon appointment. To cut production costs by 70% and test 4x more angles, I combined Midjourney & AI-generated clinical b-roll (scalp scans, consultation shots) with relatable, conversational Hinglish voiceovers addressing real elevator-mirror moments.
