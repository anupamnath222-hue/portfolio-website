The 4-Step Creative Strategy Engine

Creative testing is not guessing. We follow an engineering discipline that transforms raw customer voice mining into predictable, high-ROAS creative winners.

01

Friction & VOC Mining

We scrape Reddit communities, Amazon 1-to-3 star competitor reviews, and post-purchase customer feedback to isolate the core psychological objections stopping cold traffic from converting.

02

Psychological Angle Matrix

We construct 8+ targeted hook angles per campaign sprint, segmenting distinct buyer motivations (e.g. The Anxious Skeptic vs. The Cause-Driven Indie Supporter).

03

Direct-Response Production

Scripting native UGC creator briefs, directing dynamic AI motion assets, editing fast visual pattern-interrupts, and enforcing tight on-image copy structures (12 to 18 words).

04

Advantage+ Creative Scaling

Winning creatives with >35% thumbstop rates and strong hold times are graduated to evergreen BAU ad sets with dynamic creative testing (DCT) to prevent ad fatigue.
