Systematic Execution ✏️ How we avoid creative fatigue

The 4-Step Creative Engine

Most agencies throw random visual ideas at the wall and pray. At TPF, we deploy a disciplined quantitative framework:

PHASE 01

Unfiltered VOC Mining

We scrape Amazon 1-star reviews, Reddit rants, and customer support tickets to extract raw buyer objections and the exact vocabulary customers use when disappointed by alternatives.

PHASE 02

12-Angle Persona Matrix

Meta uses OCR and speech transcription to find buyers. We script 12 distinct angles—Cost Arbitrage, Vanity, Skepticism Killer, Social Proof—to target different personas from one ad set.

PHASE 03

Multi-Format Production

We build high-contrast statics for low-CAC bottom-funnel purchases, native vertical UGC reels for consideration, and viral meme DR hooks for impulse checkout.

PHASE 04

Fatigue Defense & Flips

When an ad wins, we iterate on the top 20%: testing 3 new visual 3-second hooks, sound flips, and static spinoffs to keep CAC low while scaling ad spend.
