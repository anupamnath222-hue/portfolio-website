The "Anti-Bacon Collar" Wash Test: Scaling Heavyweight 240 GSM Streetwear

⚡ [01] THE CHALLENGE & ACCOUNT FRICTION
Frankly Wearing crafted premium 240 GSM heavy cotton t-shirts, but cold Meta traffic blended them with fast-fashion apparel. Standard product carousels suffered from a poor 14% thumbstop rate because shoppers couldn't feel fabric density or collar ribbing quality through their phone screens.
🎯 [02] CUSTOMER FRICTION & THE HOOK ANGLE
We mined Reddit streetwear threads and customer reviews for the unspoken friction: wavy, warped collar ribbing after 3 washes ("bacon collar syndrome"). We storyboarded a split-screen UGC torture test: violently stretching the collar alongside an ordinary ₹399 tee, followed by a live washing machine stress test.
📈 [03] PERFORMANCE SCALE
The visual pattern-interrupt lifted 3-second hook retention from 14.2% to 41.8%. Meta Advantage+ allocated the majority of the account's scaling budget to this winner, holding a 3.6x blended ROAS.

The 10-Day Self-Watering Mechanism: Overcoming the Fear of Plant Casualties

⚡ [01] THE CHALLENGE & ACCOUNT FRICTION
Kyari built strong brand awareness after pitching on Shark Tank India. However, cold Meta campaigns hit a barrier at checkout. Potential plant parents abandoned their carts due to a persistent fear: "I love green spaces, but every plant I buy dies within two weeks because I forget watering schedules."
🎯 [02] CUSTOMER FRICTION & THE HOOK ANGLE
Instead of generic aesthetic photos, we engineered an infographic direct-response asset clearly breaking down the 10-day self-watering sub-irrigation mechanism. We illustrated the reservoir feeding water directly to roots on demand, killing the anxiety of over- or under-watering.
📈 [03] PERFORMANCE SCALE
Demystifying the sub-irrigation mechanism directly destroyed the core psychological objection, dropping customer acquisition costs by 29% and driving a 3.4x ROAS across first-time apartment buyers.

The 4-Step Creative Strategy Engine

Creative testing is not guessing. We follow a structured direct-response operating engine that transforms raw customer review mining into predictable, high-ROAS creative winners.

01

Friction & VOC Mining

We scrape Reddit communities, Amazon 1-to-3 star competitor reviews, and post-purchase customer feedback to isolate the core psychological objections stopping cold traffic from converting.

02

Psychological Angle Matrix

We construct 8+ targeted hook angles per campaign sprint, segmenting distinct buyer motivations (e.g. The Anxious Skeptic vs. The Problem-Aware Giver).

03

Direct-Response Production

Scripting native UGC creator briefs, directing real-world torture tests, editing fast visual pattern-interrupts, and enforcing tight on-image copy structures (12 to 18 words).

04

Advantage+ Creative Scaling

Winning creatives with >35% thumbstop rates and strong hold times are graduated to evergreen BAU ad sets with dynamic creative testing (DCT) to prevent ad fatigue.
