The "Anti-Bacon Collar" Wash Test & Multi-Persona Creator Drop Engine

⚡ [01] THE KICKOFF & BOTTLENECK
Frankly Wearing manufactured superior 240 GSM heavy cotton t-shirts with reinforced ribbing, but their Meta ad account was trapped at high customer acquisition costs. Standard apparel carousels and static catalog product shots blended into fast-fashion competitors, driving a sluggish 14% thumbstop rate because shoppers couldn't feel fabric weight or durability through a screen.
🎯 [02] THE STRATEGY & ANGLE HYPOTHESIS
We mined Reddit fashion threads and customer complaints across fast-fashion brands, uncovering the #1 unspoken friction: wavy, deformed collars after 3 washes ("bacon collar syndrome"). We engineered a split direct-response UGC torture test: violently stretching the collar alongside an ordinary ₹399 tee, followed by a washing machine speed test. For new creator drops, we deployed multi-persona angle segmentation separating Gift Givers for Niche Obsessives from Indie Art Supporters.
📈 [03] THE OUTCOME & SUSTAINED LIFT
The "Anti-Bacon Collar" torture test lifted 3-second hook retention from 14.2% to 41.8% (+148% lift). Meta Advantage+ quickly allocated 65% of the account's budget to this winner, scaling spend seamlessly while holding a 3.6x blended ROAS.

Repurposing UGC & Killing the "I Kill Every Plant I Buy" Friction

⚡ [01] THE KICKOFF & BOTTLENECK
Kyari secured major national recognition on Shark Tank India for its self-watering planters and curated indoor plants. However, post-broadcast Meta ad performance plateaued. Potential plant parents abandoned checkout due to a persistent psychological roadblock: "I love green spaces, but my plants always die within two weeks because I forget watering schedules." Generic product footage failed to demonstrate the self-watering mechanism clearly.
🎯 [02] THE STRATEGY & ANGLE HYPOTHESIS
We audited Kyari's studio shoots and repurposed existing UGC footage into a tight direct-response script: "The Plant That Refuses to Die." We opened with a relatable 1.5-second pain point: brown dying leaves in an apartment living room. Then, we demonstrated the water reservoir and detachable grow pot in 3 seconds, demonstrating how sub-irrigation delivers water directly to roots for 10 days without manual watering.
📈 [03] THE OUTCOME & SUSTAINED LIFT
The repurposed video hook drove a 38.6% thumbstop rate and dropped customer acquisition cost by 29%. The self-watering mechanism angle unlocked scale across first-time plant buyers who had previously hesitated.

Hyper-Local City Direct Response: Selling Professional Credibility at Home

⚡ [01] THE KICKOFF & BOTTLENECK
Tata Play Fiber operated in an ultra-competitive broadband market where generic ads promoted "Unlimited Fast Wi-Fi" and raw megabits per second. High customer acquisition costs and low form-fill intent plagued campaigns because consumers perceived home internet as an interchangeable commodity, ignoring standard corporate fiber ads.
🎯 [02] THE STRATEGY & ANGLE HYPOTHESIS
We shifted positioning from technical speeds to emotional stakes: home internet is career insurance for WFH professionals and modern family infrastructure. We created hyper-local geo-targeted video variations calling out specific neighborhoods across Jaipur, Navi Mumbai, Kalyan-Dombivli, and Kharghar. The hook targeted micro-stress: "Your client presentation froze for 4 seconds today. Don't let your ISP ruin your professional reputation."
📈 [03] THE OUTCOME & SUSTAINED LIFT
Hyper-local callouts produced a +162% increase in qualified broadband installation requests, cutting cost per lead by 38%. Localized neighborhood creative variants became the core acquisition engine for regional city rollouts.

The 4-Step Creative Strategy Engine

Creative testing is not guessing. We follow a systematic 4-step framework to diagnose account friction, engineer psychographic hooks, deploy multi-variant tests, and scale winners evergreen.

01

Friction & VOC Mining

We scrape Reddit communities, Amazon 1-to-3 star reviews, and post-purchase customer feedback to isolate the core psychological objections stopping cold traffic from converting.

02

Persona & Angle Matrix

We map products against distinct buyer personas (e.g. The Anxious Skeptic vs. The Social Giver) and construct 8+ targeted hook angles per campaign sprint.

03

High-Velocity Production

Directing authentic creator UGC, scripting direct-response torture tests, editing fast-paced native hooks, and engineering AI motion visual pattern-interrupts.

04

Advantage+ Evergreen Scale

Winning creatives with >30% thumbstop rates and profitable CAC are graduated to evergreen BAU ad sets with budget auto-scaling and continuous dynamic creative testing (DCT).
