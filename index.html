*** Methodology

How We Engineer Creatives That Actually Scale

Most agencies throw random visual ideas at the wall and pray. At TPF, we follow a rigorous 4-step quantitative creative engine that feeds Meta's Advantage+ algorithm distinct buyer psychologies.

PHASE 01

Unfiltered VOC Mining

We scrape Amazon 1-star reviews, Reddit rants, and customer support tickets to extract raw buyer objections and the exact vocabulary customers use when they feel disappointed by market alternatives.

PHASE 02

12-Angle Persona Matrix

Meta's algorithm uses OCR and audio transcription to find buyers. We script 12 distinct angles—Cost Arbitrage, Aesthetic Vanity, Skepticism Killer, Social Proof, and Relatability—to target different personas from one ad set.

PHASE 03

Multi-Format Production

We don't gamble on one hero video. We build high-contrast statics for low-CAC conversions, native vertical Reels for top-of-funnel consideration, and viral meme DR hooks for impulse checkout.

PHASE 04

Fatigue Defense & Flips

When an ad wins, we don't let it burn out. We iterate on the top 20%: testing 3 new visual hooks, sound flips, and static carousel spinoffs to keep CAC low while scaling ad spend.

Hook Variation Matrix

We produce 1 winning core message and test 4 distinct 3-second hooks: Sensory ASMR, Price Contrast, Direct Friction, and Relatable Meme. The winning hook unlocks 3x more watch time.

Optimized Hook Variation: 42.8% (+132% lift)

Test 02 · Audience Qualification

Angle Diversification

Single-angle ad accounts hit a ceiling within 14 days. By deploying 4 separate psychological entry points, we reach completely distinct buyer segments without ad fatigue.

Single-Angle Ad Lifespan: 12–14 days
4-Angle Matrix Lifespan: 60+ days without fatigue
Scale Factor: 2.5x ad spend unlocked

Format Balancing

Reels drive top-of-funnel consideration; high-contrast statics drive low-CAC bottom-of-funnel purchases. Balancing both in Advantage+ Shopping creates maximum blended ROAS.
