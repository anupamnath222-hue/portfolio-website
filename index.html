Scaling Past Catalog Fatigue: Building a 14-Ad BAU Creative Matrix Across 5 Product Lines

The Account Bottleneck

HOKIPO was experiencing severe ad fatigue on standard product catalog ads. Ad spend was stalling at ₹1.5L/mo with rising CAC. They needed evergreen (BAU) creative angles that converted cold traffic without margin-crushing discounts.

The Strategic Intervention

Through customer review mining, I identified the real consumer tension: solid wooden sofa frames outlive upholstery, but carpenter reupholstery costs ₹18,000 and takes two messy weeks. We built the "Reupholstery Cost Arbitrage" and "Anti-Retuck Stability" angles across 5 product lines (Sofa Covers, Cushions, Mattress Protectors, Dining Chairs, Recliners).
