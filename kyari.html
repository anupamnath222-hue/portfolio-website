02 / Repurposing Architecture

The 3 Winning Repurposed Angles & Live Voiceovers

Instead of expensive studio shoots, Anupam mined Kyari's existing B-roll stock archive and recut it with high-retention direct-response scripts. Tap "Unmute Voiceover" on any video to hear the actual recorded pacing.

Top of Funnel Repurpose 02

The 3-Light Matchmaker

"Here are three houseplants you can choose based on how much light you get."

Kills the #1 reason people hesitate: "My room is too dark for plants." Gives instant utility and categorizes Areca Palm (bright), Sansevieria (low light), and Peace Lily (soft light).

Recorded Voiceover Script ⏱ 40.6s

"Bright indirect light? That's where Areca Palm works beautifully. Low light? Sansevieria Golden can tolerate it. Softer indirect? Peace Lily. With Kyari's self-watering system, just fill the reservoir and the plant draws water as needed."

Middle of Funnel Repurpose 03

The Amazon Faux Intercept

"I was this close to ordering a faux plant on Amazon, but then I found Kyari..."

Competitor redirect angle. Captures buyers actively browsing artificial plastic decor and convinces them that Kyari's ZZ Green with metal pot offers real nature with zero watering stress.

Recorded Voiceover Script ⏱ 47.0s

"We wanted to add some life to our living room space. It's a real plant, but the self-watering pot makes it so much easier to maintain. Plus, I love how the lush green foliage and metal pot look. 100% recommend."

Bottom of Funnel Repurpose 05

Shark Tank & 6-Month Proof

"Before you buy a plant online, see what mine looks like after 6 months."

Destroys delivery & longevity skepticism. Demonstrates real new leaf growth half a year later and leverages Shark Tank validation to convert cart abandoners into buyers.

Recorded Voiceover Script ⏱ 38.0s

"I remember seeing Kyari on Shark Tank, so I finally decided to try one myself. This is what it looks like today—new leaves, looking healthier. Seeing a plant actually thrive months after you bought it is the best review."
